Open RotMG Gold

You cannot buy Gold in Open RotMG.

One-time Gold

  • 100 : Link Discord - use /link in Discord.
  • 100 : Get into the top 50% of the Collection Log hiscores.
  • 50 : Submit your first bug report - use Report bug on the website.
  • 75 : Reach Blue Star rank (13 stars).
  • 150 : Reach Red Star rank (26 stars).
  • 250 : Reach Orange Star rank (39 stars).
  • 750 : Reach Yellow Star rank (52 stars).
  • 2500 : Reach White Star rank (65 stars).
  • 100 : Make a YouTube video about Open RotMG.

Each star-rank reward is paid once per account. Existing ranks count, too.

Repeatable Gold

  • 100 : Per 1,000 views on a YouTube video about Open RotMG.
  • 50 : Per 1,000 views on a Short or a video on another social media platform.

Trade Fame

  • Use /swap in game to trade Fame for Gold, 2:1.
